# همراه حفظ قرآن — Design brief

- Design read: A calm, trustworthy private learning space for Quran memorizers and their teachers, built for daily consistency rather than competition.
- Concept spine: The illuminated study desk — each class is a quiet circle of learning, with progress gathered like pages in a personal mushaf.
- Delivery tier: editorial dashboard with restrained micro-interactions.
- Animation mode: non-animated — user chose a simple professional design with subtle animations; use micro-interactions, no cinematic camera journey.
- Locked palette: forest green #174B3B, deep green #10392F, warm ivory #F7F4EA, paper #FFFDF7, muted sage #DCE7DA, antique gold #B68A42, ink #26362E. A quiet botanical palette supports focus and avoids high-contrast gamification.
- Locked type: Vazirmatn for Persian UI, system sans fallback. Clear Persian numerals and generous line-height.
- Brand constraints: Original identity for «همراه حفظ قرآن»; no existing logo or font supplied. RTL-first, mobile-first, professional, understated.
- Audience: Private group of memorizers, teachers, and one administrator.
- Core roles: admin, teacher, memorizer. Teachers see only their assigned classes; memorizers see only their own reports and class assignments.
- Information architecture: dashboard, classes, memorization reports, assignments, audio review, feedback, and member management.
- Section plan: app shell; overview metrics; today's class activity; pending reviews; weekly progress; next assignment.
- Asset plan: simple typographic/monogram identity, inline botanical geometry, custom favicon, branded launch cover. Dashboard data visualizations are native UI, not decorative stock imagery.
- CTA inventory: «ثبت گزارش حفظ» (primary memorizer action), «بررسی گزارش‌ها» (teacher action), «ایجاد کلاس» (admin action), «مشاهده برنامه» (secondary action).
- Interaction rules: short transitions on navigation, hover/focus, and progress updates; respect reduced motion; no autoplay or distracting effects.
- Privacy: invite-only membership, server-verified roles, per-class authorization, private audio storage, no public member directory.
